Example Employee Leave Policy

This is a fictional document for testing the document chatbot.

Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Public holidays are provided in addition to this allowance.
Part-time employees receive a proportional annual leave allowance.

Requesting leave
Employees should submit annual leave requests to their manager at least two weeks in advance.
Managers approve requests based on team coverage and operational needs.

Unused leave
Employees can carry over up to five unused annual leave days into the next calendar year.
Carried-over days must be used by March 31.

This policy does not specify salaries, pension benefits, or sick leave allowances.
